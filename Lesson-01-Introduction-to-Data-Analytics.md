Introduction to Data Analytics

Overview

This lesson introduced me to Data Analytics, its importance, and how data can be used to answer questions and support decision-making.

Learning Objectives

	•	Understand the meaning of Data Analytics.
	•	Learn why businesses use data to make decisions.
	•	Understand the four major types of Data Analytics.
	•	Explore how Data Analytics can solve real-world problems.

What I Learned

I learned that Data Analytics involves examining, cleaning, transforming, and interpreting data to discover useful information and support decision-making.

Questions a Data Analyst Can Investigate

 Data Analytics involves asking questions that help us understand a situation or solve a problem.

Some examples include:

	•	What happened?
	•	Why did it happen?
	•	What might happen in the future?
	•	What actions can be taken to improve the situation?

I also learned about four major types of Data Analytics:

	1.	Descriptive Analytics: Explains what happened.
	2.	Diagnostic Analytics: Investigates why something happened.
	3.	Predictive Analytics: Estimates what may happen in the future.
	4.	Prescriptive Analytics: Recommends actions that could help achieve a desired outcome.

Findings and Observations

One thing I observed is that many modern business organizations use real-time data to monitor activities and support timely decision-making. However, this does not mean historical data is less useful in analysis. Historical data remains valuable because it helps organizations identify long-term trends, recognize recurring patterns, compare past and present performance, and make informed decisions.

I also learned that traditional analytics often focuses on descriptive analytics, using historical data to understand what has happened. By examining this data, organizations can identify trends, uncover patterns, generate insights, and use those insights to guide business decisions.

This helped me understand that both real-time and historical data are valuable. Real-time data helps organizations understand what is happening now, while historical data helps them understand past performance and identify patterns over time.

Although traditional analytics is not limited to descriptive analytics, although descriptive analytics is a common part of traditional reporting. Traditional analytical methods can also include diagnostic techniques that investigate why something happened.

Key Takeaways

	•	Data Analytics helps transform raw data into useful information.
	•	Business decisions should be supported by evidence from data.
	•	The right interpretation depends on the question being investigated.
	•	Findings should be communicated clearly to support decision-making.

Challenges Faced

One of the things I needed to understand was the difference between the types of Data Analytics and how they apply to real business situations.

My Learning Reflection

This introductory lesson has helped me understand the meaning and purpose of Data Analytics and the different ways it can be used to investigate problems and support decisions.
